Our Kitchen

A neighbourhood table with an Italian heart

The Fanus Restaurant brings together the comfort of classic Italian cooking and the easy-going spirit of a local diner. From slow-cooked beef ragù and creamy risottos to buttermilk-fried chicken burgers, everything is prepared to order and served generously.

Whether it's a quick lunch, a family dinner, or a takeaway night in — you'll find familiar flavours, fair prices, and a warm welcome on Albany Highway.
