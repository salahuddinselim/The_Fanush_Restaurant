Authentic Cucina Italiana

A neighbourhood table with a generous Italian heart

The Fanus Restaurant brings together time-honoured Italian culinary craftsmanship with the warm, relaxed hospitality of Western Australia. From 6-hour braised beef ragù and handmade gnocchi to crisp wood-fired pizza and golden chicken parmigianas, every dish is cooked to order from scratch.

Whether you are dining in for a romantic dinner, gathering the whole family for Sunday lunch, or collecting a Friday night takeaway feast — you will always receive generous portions, honest prices, and a welcoming smile on Albany Highway.
